Thinking about booking microblading but not sure what actually happens on the day? Here is a simple walkthrough of your appointment at Tiger's Eyes, so you know exactly what to expect from start to finish.

## 1. Your consultation

Every treatment begins with a relaxed consultation. We talk through the shape, colour and thickness you are hoping for, and I map out a bespoke brow design to suit your face shape and natural features. Nothing starts until you are completely happy with the shape.

## 2. The treatment

Using fine, precise strokes, I create hair-like lines that blend naturally with your own brows. A numbing cream keeps you comfortable throughout. Most appointments take around two hours, and you are welcome to relax the whole time.

## 3. Healing

Your brows will look bolder for the first few days, then soften as they heal over the following two weeks. I will give you full aftercare instructions to keep them looking their best while the skin settles.

## 4. Your top-up

A complimentary top-up around 6–8 weeks later perfects the colour and fills any areas that have faded during healing. This is included in your £250 full treatment.
